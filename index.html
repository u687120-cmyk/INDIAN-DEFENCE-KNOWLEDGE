Career Pathways

How To Join

A study-first guide to officer and Agniveer entries. Dates, age cut-offs, vacancies, physical standards, and medical rules can change every notification cycle.

Selection Flow

  • Read the latest official notification before applying.
  • Check age, education, gender, marital status, height, eyesight, and medical standards.
  • Apply online through official portals only.
  • Clear written exam or shortlisting stage.
  • Clear SSB or AFSB where applicable, then medicals and merit list.
  • Report to the allotted academy or training centre.
